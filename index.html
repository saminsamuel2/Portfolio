6-Month Keyboard Course (Beginner Level)

This 6-month Keyboard Course is designed for beginners of all ages and focuses on building strong musical foundations in a simple and enjoyable way. The course includes 8 classes per month, totaling 48 structured classes, along with 2 additional free revision/practice sessions, making a total of 50 classes.

Students will begin with nursery rhymes and basic melodies to develop rhythm, finger coordination, and confidence on the keyboard. The syllabus gradually introduces scales and chords, helping students understand essential music concepts and improve hand technique. Learners will also practice playing complete songs to enhance musical expression and timing.

Students master full versions of these beloved songs with proper timing and expression:

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your child's musical journey.

Piano Students: A digital/electric piano is suitable from beginner to intermediate level. For Grade 6–8 and above, I recommend upgrading to an acoustic piano.

Keyboard Students: A 61-key electronic keyboard is suitable for beginners. For Grade 6–8 and above, I recommend upgrading to a professional keyboard.

The ideal minimum starting age is 8 years old. At this age, children possess the finger strength, hand span, and focus needed to grasp basic note identification and rhythm.

We specialize in students aged 8 to 15, adapting our teaching style to their developmental stage. For 8-year-olds, our Weekly Half-Hour Plan (25 AED) is specifically crafted to keep lessons engaging, playful, and fatigue-free.

With over 15,000 lessons taught both online and offline, our online teaching methodology has been perfected over 15 years to match—and often exceed—the effectiveness of in-person lessons.

Students learn both theory and practical skills, with a strong focus on reading music notation. Once students can read music confidently, they can learn and progress easily through online lessons.

We understand that school schedules, exams, and family events happen! If you notify us at least 24 hours in advance, we will gladly reschedule the class to an alternate mutually convenient makeup slot.

In our signature 6-Month Keyboard Course, we even include 2 additional free revision/practice sessions explicitly built in to ensure no student ever falls behind on their curriculum.
